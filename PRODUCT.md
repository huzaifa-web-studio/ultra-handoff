# Handoff

## Product Overview

**Handoff** is a client-facing project delivery and approval platform designed for agencies, freelancers, and service-based teams.

It provides a shared workspace where service providers and their clients can agree on project scope, communicate expectations, review completed work, and formally approve deliveries.

Handoff is not intended to replace project management tools. Instead, it focuses on the relationship between the team delivering the work and the client receiving it.

## The Problem

Client projects often become difficult to manage because important decisions are scattered across emails, messaging apps, meetings, and documents.

This creates several problems:

- Unclear expectations about what was agreed upon.
- Scope changes that are difficult to track.
- Feedback and revision requests spread across multiple conversations.
- No clear record of what was delivered and approved.
- Disagreements about responsibilities, completion, and acceptance.

Traditional project management tools primarily focus on internal tasks and team productivity. Handoff focuses on the client-facing side of project delivery.

## Our Solution

Handoff creates a structured process around two essential agreements:

**Scope Approval:** Before work begins, the agency defines what will be delivered. The client reviews the proposed scope, requests adjustments if necessary, and formally approves the agreed work.

**Delivery Approval:** Once the work is completed, the agency submits its deliverables for review. The client can approve the delivery or request corrections until the agreed requirements are satisfied.

Throughout this process, Handoff keeps discussions, supporting files, decisions, and previous submissions connected to the relevant work.

## Who It's For

Handoff is primarily designed for:

- Web development and software agencies.
- Design and creative studios.
- Freelancers delivering client projects.
- Digital service providers.
- Small teams managing multiple client engagements.

## Core Product Principles

1. **Clarity:** Both parties should always understand what is expected and what has been agreed upon.
2. **Accountability:** Important approvals, requests, and decisions should have a clear record.
3. **Transparency:** Clients should be able to understand project progress without accessing the agency's internal task management.
4. **Simplicity:** The experience should feel straightforward for both technical and non-technical clients.
5. **Trust:** Formal agreements and documented deliveries should reduce misunderstandings and strengthen professional relationships.

## Why We're Building Handoff

Handoff is being built to solve a practical problem in client service delivery: the gap between managing work internally and managing expectations externally.

Our goal is to give agencies a professional, reliable way to define commitments, demonstrate completed work, and obtain clear client acceptance.

Initially, Handoff will also serve as a real-world SaaS product demonstrating our ability to design, build, and deliver complete software solutions.

Long term, we want Handoff to become a useful standalone product that agencies and freelancers can rely on throughout their client relationships.

## Product Vision

**Make every client engagement clear, accountable, and confidently delivered.**

Handoff should become the place where agencies and clients know exactly what was promised, what was delivered, what needs attention, and what has been approved.
